HATCHGUARD-ML

A Self-Regulated Hatchery System for Monitoring, Feeding, and Predicting Survivability of Giant Freshwater Prawn Larvae in a Controlled Environment

About HATCHGUARD-ML System

HATCHGUARD-ML revolutionizes freshwater prawn hatchery management through intelligent automation and predictive analytics, ensuring optimal growth conditions and maximum survivability rates.

Our innovative system integrates advanced machine learning algorithms with real-time environmental monitoring to create the perfect controlled environment for giant freshwater prawn larvae. By continuously analyzing water quality, temperature, pH levels, and feeding patterns, HATCHGUARD-ML automatically adjusts conditions to maximize growth and survival rates.

The system's predictive capabilities allow hatchery operators to anticipate potential issues before they occur, reducing losses and increasing overall productivity. Our automated feeding system ensures precise nutrition delivery at optimal intervals, eliminating human error and inconsistency.

System Features

  • Real-time water quality monitoring and alerts
  • Automated feeding with portion control
  • Machine learning-based survivability prediction
  • Self-regulating environmental controls
  • Comprehensive data analytics dashboard
  • Web app for remote monitoring
  • Automated health assessment of larvae
  • Customizable growth optimization algorithms
