POST-LARVAI

A Self-Regulated System for Monitoring, Feeding, and Predicting Future Status of Giant Freshwater Prawn Post-Larvae in a Controlled Environment

About POST-LARVAI System

POST-LARVAI revolutionizes freshwater prawn hatchery management through intelligent automation and predictive analytics, ensuring optimal growth conditions and maximum survivability rates.

Our innovative system integrates advanced machine learning algorithms with real-time environmental monitoring to create the perfect controlled environment for giant freshwater prawn post-larvae. By continuously analyzing water quality such as temperature, pH, DO, ammonia, turbidity, nitrite levels, and feeding patterns, POST-LARVAI automatically adjusts conditions to maximize growth and survival rates.

System Features

  • Real-time water quality monitoring and alerts
  • Automated feeding with portion control
  • Machine learning-based survivability prediction
  • Self-regulating environmental controls
  • Comprehensive data analytics dashboard
