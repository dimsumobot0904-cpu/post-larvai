System Tutorial & User Guide

Learn how to use the POST-LARVAI Water Monitoring System.

Alamin kung paano gamitin ang POST-LARVAI Water Monitoring System.

1

Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard shows the overall status of the system. It displays the real-time status of the sensors and relays that control the water environment.

Sa Dashboard makikita ang pangkalahatang estado ng system. Dito naka-display ang real-time status ng mga sensors at relays na nagkokontrol sa water environment.

2

Sensors Data Monitoring

Go to **Sensors Data** to monitor the pH level, Dissolved Oxygen (DO), Temperature, and Turbidity in the water containing giant freshwater prawn post-larvae.

Pumunta sa **Sensors Data** para masubaybayan ang pH level, Dissolved Oxygen (DO), Temperature, at Turbidity sa tubigan ng giant freshwater prawn post-larvae.

Recommended Water Parameters for Ulang Larvae

  • Temperature: 28°C - 31°C
  • pH Level: 7.5 - 8.5 (Optimal: 7.8 - 8.2)
  • Dissolved Oxygen (DO): > 5.0 mg/L
  • Nitrite: 0.01 - 0.25
  • Ammonia: > 0.1 ppm
  • Turbidity: > (aalamin pa)
